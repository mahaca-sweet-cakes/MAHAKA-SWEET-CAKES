Before you pay

How to place the order

Always confirm the order first. We send the exact amount and the payment details in the same WhatsApp chat.

Send your order

Message us on WhatsApp with the item code, the date and the message for the cake.

Get written confirmation

We reply with the final price, the delivery fee and the collection or delivery time.

Pay using one method below

Send us the transaction reference on WhatsApp so we can check it against our own records.

Keeping it safe

How we confirm a payment

We never accept a screenshot alone as proof of payment. A screenshot can be edited or can show a transfer that was later reversed. Instead, the person handling payments checks our own mobile money or bank record and matches the transaction ID, the amount and the sender to the order.

Only after that check do we mark the order as paid and begin baking. If we cannot find the payment, we tell the customer within the same day.

Our refund rule

You can cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before the agreed collection or delivery time, and we refund what you paid.

Once baking has started, the order cannot be refunded, because the ingredients are already used. If something is wrong with the cake, tell us on the day you receive it and we will replace it or refund you.

We do not accept cryptocurrency. Bank of Uganda does not recognise cryptocurrency as legal tender in Uganda and has warned the public about the risks. For a small student business, mobile money and bank transfer are simpler, traceable and safer for both the customer and us.
