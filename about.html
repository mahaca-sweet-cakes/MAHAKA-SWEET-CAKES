Our story

Six students, one kitchen, one name

MAHACA Sweet Cakes is a student business built at MUBS Jinja Campus by six third-year Bachelor of Entrepreneurship students who wanted to make celebrations on campus a little sweeter.

  • 6 founders
  • MUBS Jinja Campus
  • Made to order
Where the name comes from

MAHACA is us

The name is not a word you will find in a dictionary. It is built from the first names of the six founders: Mebra, Allan, Hazrat, Aisha, Christine and Alvin.

We started MAHACA because buying a good cake on campus was harder than it should be. Students had to travel into Jinja town, order days in advance, or settle for whatever was available. We wanted a simple option: see the cakes and prices online, send one message, and collect a fresh cake on campus.

Everything we sell is made to order. We bake in small batches, decorate by hand, and confirm every detail with the customer in writing before we start. Our tagline says the rest: Bake. Celebrate. Enjoy.

The six founders, MUBS Jinja Campus

What we do

Cakes for campus life

We serve two kinds of customers: individuals celebrating something, and groups ordering for a meeting or an event.

For individuals

Birthday cakes, graduation cakes and small celebration cakes for students, lecturers and staff, in sizes from six to fifteen servings.

For groups and offices

Bulk cakes, cupcake boxes and sheet cakes for departments, clubs, class groups and campus event organisers, quoted in writing.

For the campus

Free collection on campus or delivery within MUBS Jinja Campus for UGX 5,000, arranged around your timetable.

What we stand for

Four rules we bake by

Honest claims only

We do not invent reviews, awards or customer numbers. If we say a cake is fresh, it is because it was baked for your order.

Clear prices

The price you see on this site is the price we quote on WhatsApp. Delivery and any extras are stated before you confirm.

Careful food handling

We bake in a clean, home-style kitchen, store ingredients properly and tell customers about allergens such as wheat, eggs, milk and nuts.

Your privacy

We only ask for the details we need to make and deliver your order. We do not sell customer information. Our full privacy notice is on the Policies page.
