The MAHACA kitchen

Every cake is baked after you order it.

Nothing waits on a shelf. We take your order, bake it in small batches and decorate it by hand on the day it is collected.

MAHACA was started by six third-year students who wanted campus celebrations to be simple: see the cakes and prices online, send one message, and collect something worth photographing.

How to order

Three steps, one WhatsApp conversation.

Choose your cake

Browse the cakes page, or our WhatsApp catalogue, and note the item code.

Message us

Send the code, the date you need it and the message you want on the cake.

Confirm and collect

We confirm the price and time in writing, then you pay and collect or receive delivery.

Ordering for a meeting, club or department?

We take bulk and event orders for class groups, student associations and university departments. Tell us the number of people and the date, and we will quote you in writing.
